# BrightSprout / Kids Activity Learning — Age 6–7 Simplification

## Goal
The activity catalogue has been redesigned for a child aged 6–7. The focus is short, concrete, visual tasks with simple instructions and familiar concepts.

## Design rules
- All 23 activities are Level 1.
- Typical activity time is 2–4 minutes.
- Most activities use 3–4 questions/rounds rather than long runs.
- Avoid advanced terminology, multi-step deduction, large calculations, fractions beyond simple halves/quarters, prime numbers, multiples, mental rotation, and abstract inference.
- Use familiar topics: numbers to 20, simple addition/subtraction, shapes, colours, animals, plants, directions, everyday words, short stories, simple clues, drawing, and rhythm copying.
- Give one clear instruction at a time.
- Prefer recognition and simple choices over typing.
- Keep mistakes low-pressure and encourage another try.
- Creative activities are open-ended and should not judge artistic quality.

## Activity redesign
1. Memory Match — 4 pairs instead of 8 pairs.
2. Copy the Pattern — 3-tap sequences.
3. Number Fun — counting, small addition, number comparison, basic shapes.
4. Share the Cake — halves, quarters, and simple sharing.
5. Quick Numbers — addition/subtraction within 10.
6. Logic Friends — simple ordering and category questions.
7. Secret Shape — simple shape clues.
8. Pattern Train — repeating colour/number/shape patterns.
9. Shape Grid — simple missing-shape sequences.
10. Spot the Different One — two short visual rounds.
11. Wait & Tap — three calm reaction rounds.
12. Draw & Create — draw a favourite animal.
13. Story Choices — three very short everyday scenes.
14. Word Match — basic synonyms, opposites, and categories.
15. Little Reader — four-sentence story with direct questions.
16. Help the Robot — simple safe/strong choices.
17. Direction Fun — up/down/left/right.
18. Nature Fun — basic plants, animals, and nature facts.
19. Build a Bridge — simple stability/safety choices.
20. Copy the Beat — three-tap visual rhythm.
21. Find the Clue — three simple observations.
22. Number Hunt — find even numbers in small ranges.
23. Brain Party — four easy mixed questions.

## Content source direction
The uploaded Sunrise 3 Activity Book was used only as a reference for age-appropriate activity concepts such as alphabet ordering, colours, counting, simple vocabulary, listening, spelling, actions, body parts, clothes, food, and basic questions. The application should continue using original content and original UI rather than copying the book's pages, artwork, characters, or exact wording.
